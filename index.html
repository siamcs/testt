FAQ

Common questions

What kinds of projects do you take on?

We work across four divisions: civil infrastructure (roads, highways, paving, structural concrete), institutional buildings such as schools and campuses, nutritional supply of fortified foods, and defense logistics including footwear, uniforms and rations.

Do you work on government tenders?

Yes. Our clients include government departments and agencies such as the Roads & Highways Department, Public Works, education authorities and disciplined forces. Send us the tender reference and we will review it with you.

Where do you operate?

Our head office is in Rampura, Banasree, Dhaka, and we deliver projects and supplies across Bangladesh.

How do I get a quote?

Use the contact form below or email info@hermanosynergy.com. Include the scope, location and timeline, and we will come back to you within one business day.

Are your supplies certified?

Our nutritional and defense supplies are sourced and packaged to meet the hygiene and certification standards each client requires. Documentation is provided with every delivery.
